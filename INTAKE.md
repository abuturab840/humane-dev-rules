# Intake questions

Answer these at the start of every project (rule 1). Every **Always** rule applies regardless. Each "yes" below also switches on the **Only if** rules listed.

| # | Question | If yes, add rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do we collect or store **personal data**? | 3 (if health, biometric or payment data), 14, 22 (Level 2 if sensitive), 26 (encryption), 45 (privacy notice), 50 (breach plan), 51 (data requests) |
| 2 | Do we have **users in the EU or UK**? | 15, 16, 19, 20 if there are optional cookies or tags |
| 3 | Do we have **users in the US**? | 17 (if we sell/share data or run targeted ads), 18 (if there is tracking) |
| 4 | Could **children** use it? | 8 (no infinite scroll for under-18s), 43 |
| 5 | Does it include **AI**? | 36a, 36b, 37, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42 (each as its condition applies) |
| 6 | Does it take **payments or subscriptions**? | 31, 45 (terms with clear cancellation) |
| 7 | Can users **post content, images or chat**? | 11 (if we rank content), 51 (48h takedowns) |
| 8 | Do we use **optional cookies, pixels or tracking**? | 15 to 20, 33 (cookie scan), 45 (cookie policy) |
| 9 | Is it a **mobile app** or installable **software product**? | 22 (MASVS for mobile), 47 (support period) |
| 10 | Do users **log in**? | 13, 23, 33 (access control tests), 45 (terms) |

Also apply, where the product has them: rule 6 (notifications), 7 (video or audio), 8 (feeds), 34 (major launch or product live a year), 57 (rewards or gamification), 58 (invites), 59 (regular use), 60 (numbers or reviews).

## Tip

Write the answers at the top of your project's README or planning doc, with the list of rules that apply. Revisit them whenever the product gains a major feature, such as accounts, payments or AI.

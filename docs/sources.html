Where the rules come from

Sources

The standards, laws and research behind each rule.

The rules draw on these standards, laws and research. Last reviewed November 2026.

01

Humane design

  • Center for Humane Technology: Humane Design Principles, AI Roadmap, Preserving What Makes Us Human, Foundations of Humane Technology course (free)
  • Consequence Scanning (Open Data Institute)
  • Deceptive Patterns catalogue
  • Calm Technology principles
  • US FTC, Bringing Dark Patterns to Light (2022)
  • EDPB Guidelines 03/2022 on deceptive design patterns
02

Security

  • NIST Secure Software Development Framework (SP 800-218)
  • OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS)
  • OWASP Mobile Application Security (MASVS / MASTG)
  • OWASP Top 10 (2025) and API Security Top 10
  • MITRE / CISA CWE Top 25 (2025)
  • SLSA, Sigstore, CycloneDX, SPDX, OpenSSF Scorecard
  • CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog
  • NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, Incident Response
  • security.txt (RFC 9116)
03

AI

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework and Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)
  • Microsoft Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction
  • C2PA Content Credentials
04

Engagement

  • Nir Eyal, Hooked (2014) and Indistractable (2019); summaries by Farnam Street and Stanford GSB
  • Eyal on the regret test: Behavioral Scientist, Quartz
  • Big Think critique of variable rewards
05

Accessibility

  • W3C WCAG 2.2
06

Law (summary only, not legal advice)

  • EU: GDPR; ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) (cookies); Digital Services Act; AI Act (transparency duties from 2 August 2026); Cyber Resilience Act (vulnerability reporting from 11 September 2026); Product Liability Directive (software covered from 9 December 2026); European Accessibility Act.
  • UK: UK GDPR; Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code); Online Safety Act.
  • US: FTC Act Section 5; COPPA (amended rule, full compliance 22 April 2026); TAKE IT DOWN Act (48-hour removals from 19 May 2026); ADA; state privacy laws (about 20 states, Global Privacy Control required in about 12); California SB 243 (companion chatbots) and other state AI laws.
  • Elsewhere: Canada (PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25), Brazil (LGPD), China (PIPL, AI labelling rules), India (DPDP Act and Rules), South Korea (AI Basic Act), Japan (APPI).

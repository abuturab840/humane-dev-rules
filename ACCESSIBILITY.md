# Accessibility

I want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or zoom.

## What I aim for

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, the same standard rule 44 asks of every project.

## What I've done

- Text and background colours meet the AA contrast levels, in both light and dark mode.
- Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
- The pages use proper headings, lists and tables, so screen readers can move around them.
- The layout works on phones and when zoomed in to 200%.
- There are no videos, animations or pop-ups.

## What I know isn't finished

The site hasn't had a full test with a screen reader yet. If something doesn't work for you, please tell me.

## Privacy on this site

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps its own server logs under its privacy statement.

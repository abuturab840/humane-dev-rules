## Law (summary only, not legal advice)
- **EU:** GDPR; ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) (cookies); Digital Services Act; AI Act (transparency duties from 2 August 2026); Cyber Resilience Act (vulnerability reporting from 11 September 2026); Product Liability Directive (software covered from 9 December 2026); European Accessibility Act.
- **UK:** UK GDPR; Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code); Online Safety Act.
- **US:** FTC Act Section 5; COPPA (amended rule, full compliance 22 April 2026); TAKE IT DOWN Act (48-hour removals from 19 May 2026); ADA; state privacy laws (about 20 states, Global Privacy Control required in about 12); California SB 243 (companion chatbots) and other state AI laws.
- **Elsewhere:** Canada (PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25), Brazil (LGPD), China (PIPL, AI labelling rules), India (DPDP Act and Rules), South Korea (AI Basic Act), Japan (APPI).
